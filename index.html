How it listens

For piano and pedalled music: a quiet melody over loud accompaniment, or harmonies blurred by the sustain pedal, are flagged less often. Choose Off for unpedalled keyboard music. This part has two staves, so it is on.

Limits, up front. Browsers stop recording when the screen locks, so keep this page open with the screen on. The app mostly hears note names. On a single-line part it also checks the octave (roughly G3 to C7; above D♯6 it can only tell that a note sounded too low) and whether repeated notes were played again rather than held. It does not judge tuning, tempo or rhythm otherwise, and it can’t follow percussion. A heavy sustain pedal blurs piano chords, so expect fewer reliable flags there. The notation preview is simplified. For a photo or PDF, convert it to MusicXML first with MuseScore or Audiveris. Audio and score stay on this device, and the latest session is kept.
